PicoCalc Space Game - game details
==================================
Author: Thomas Dzubin

Lifted from the header comment of space_game.c (which now carries only a
short summary).  Covers startup, the full control map, the status strip, the
universe model, aliens, the flight model, autopilot, rendering and audio.

Startup
-------
Before every game (the first one and every restart after GAME OVER) the
start screen shows: the big title ("YET ANOTHER SPACE ALIEN GAME (YASAG)",
by Thomas Dzubin - drawn by big_text(), which blows the 8x10 font up into
blocks since there is no large font) and, on the lines below it, the four
difficulty choices.  On the very first showing (program startup) it also plays
the play_win_song() jingle.  splash_screen() waits for 0..3 and returns it to
run_game():
    0  No aliens - just the ship in an empty universe (still has the "Arrow
       Keys to Move" / "Press Q to Quit" HINTS)
    1  Easy Aliens (with hints) - 20 aliens; they spawn but never move / spot /
       hunt (still shootable / rammable); on-screen HINTS help you (see HINTS)
    2  Regular Aliens - 40 aliens; they drift, then hunt once they see the ship
    3  Hard Aliens - 60 aliens; mass attack (every alien in the universe charges
       the ship at once, no line of sight needed), and torpedoes re-fire twice
       as fast as levels 1-2 (a cloaked ship is still unseen)
run_game() sets alien_max = ALIEN_MAX_L1/L2/L3 (20/40/60) from the level
(ALIEN_MAX_INIT 50 for level 0).
The level shows as "Lv N" in the right strip, above the kill counter, with a
MM:SS COUNTDOWN clock beside it: it starts at 15:00 (GAME_LEN_S = 900 s, the
same on every level), ticks DOWN in whole seconds, freezes while the game is
paused, and at 00:00 the game is over.  Level 0 has no other end condition.
Then a CONTROLS legend screen shows the key map with a 10 -> 0 countdown
along the bottom; the game starts at 0, or on any key press.

Controls (raw south-bridge keyboard FIFO - we do NOT call picocalc_init()):
    LEFT / RIGHT arrow  - rotate the hull in place.  A white attitude jet
                          flares off the front of the side OPPOSITE the turn
                          and a hiss plays while held; fuel burns by time
                          held, not per key press.  Orientation only: turning
                          never bends the ship's existing travel.
    UP arrow            - fire the main engine (needs fuel): one velocity
                          impulse along the nose per DISTINCT key-down
                          (holding it does nothing - auto-repeat is
                          ignored), capped at SPEED_MAX.  The big orange
                          tail flame shows for ~FLAME_HOLD_MS after the
                          press, then the ship coasts (no drag in vacuum).
    DOWN arrow          - the MIRROR of UP (needs fuel): one impulse per
                          distinct key-down along the OPPOSITE of the nose,
                          at half the strength of an UP push.  Not a brake -
                          from rest it flies you backwards at half speed.
                          Two small white flames show off the front.
    SPACE              - fire a torpedo from the nose (needs ammo), opposite
                          the flame.  One per press; up to MAX_TORPEDOES on
                          screen; each flies straight to the box edge and
                          vanishes.
    S                  - raise the shield (costs SHIELD_PWR_COST Power, one
                          off - so it can't go up without that much power):
                          level jumps to SHIELD_START (100).  The hull turns
                          cyan + a hex ring is drawn.  The level only falls,
                          to hits: -SHIELD_HIT_TORPEDO per alien torpedo
                          blocked, -SHIELD_HIT_RAM per alien ram (the alien
                          still dies).  At 0 the shield is down and hull
                          DAMAGE starts to accrue.  A second S drops it
                          manually (no Power back).  Shown as its own cyan
                          gauge (4th bar).
    C                  - toggle the CLOAK (needs power to engage).  On: the
                          hull vanishes and aliens lose the ship (any that
                          were hunting keep drifting on their last heading).
                          While cloaked the ship CANNOT fire or raise the
                          shield, thrust is capped at the slowest crawl, and
                          CLOAK_DRAIN power drains every second.  Drops when
                          Power hits 0.  "CLOAKED" shows in the readout column.
    ENTER              - "flip and burn" (needs fuel + some speed).  The
                          ship visibly swings round until the nose faces
                          RETROGRADE (main engine toward the direction of
                          travel), then burns the main engine to a stop.
                          It IS an autopilot: manual keys lock out and the
                          one AUTOPILOT banner shows "AUTOPILOT" /
                          "flip-and-burn"; when it finishes the banner
                          reverts to whatever mode you were in (AUTOPILOT,
                          or a brief "MANUAL" flash).  Takes long enough that
                          an alien can hit you mid-flip.
    ?                  - pause: freeze the game and show the CONTROLS legend
                          until any key is pressed, then resume.
    A                  - toggle AUTOPILOT (see below).  A second A leaves
                          it; so does M (back to MANUAL nav, the default).
                          Either aborts a hop in progress.
    (in AUTOPILOT)     - the four ARROW keys each launch a one-box autopilot
                          "hop" in that screen direction: turn to face it,
                          fire the engine to AUTO_CRUISE, coast, then
                          retro-burn to a dead stop exactly AUTO_DIST px
                          away and snap onto that line.  J / K hand-rotate
                          the hull CCW / CW between hops.  Manual flight keys
                          are locked while a hop runs - EXCEPT ENTER, which
                          still bails you out into a flip-and-burn (handy
                          when the hop is about to run you into an alien).
                          The AUTOPILOT banner shows in the box; leaving it
                          flashes "MANUAL" briefly.
    ESC                - quit this game and go back to the start screen
                          (splash + difficulty pick).  Immediate, no prompt.
    Q                  - quit: freezes the game and asks "Quit?"; Y goes back
                          to the start screen (like Esc), any other key cancels.
                          It does NOT reboot - only ~ does that.
    ~                  - reboot into BOOTSEL (USB mass-storage) mode.
                          ~ is Shift + backtick; needing the two-key combo
                          keeps a stray backtick from dropping to BOOTSEL.
                          NOT shown on the in-game CONTROLS legend.

Status strip (the gauges + readouts)
-----------------------------------
FIVE short bars stacked FLUSH (no gap) in the strip below the box - Fuel,
Power, Ammo, Shield, Damage - each a coloured bar (for the graph readers)
plus a text readout beside it (for the text readers).  Fuel / Power / Shield
/ Damage read as a bare "0..100%" (the Damage bar FILLS as you take hits);
Ammo keeps its "cur/max" count.  The Lv / Kills / CLOAKED readouts sit in a
column to the right of those (at level 0, "No Aliens" replaces the kill
count).  No speed bar.
    Fuel  - starts FUEL_MAX, spent manoeuvring: FUEL_COST_FWD per distinct
            UP key-down, FUEL_COST_REV per distinct DOWN key-down (auto-
            repeat is ignored for both), and -1 per FUEL_ROT_FRAMES frames
            spent actually turning with LEFT / RIGHT (charged by time held,
            not per key event, so it doesn't depend on the keyboard's
            repeat rate; the flip-and-burn flip pays the same way).  At
            0 EVERYTHING stops - no forward/reverse thrust, no turning; you
            just coast on whatever velocity you had.
    Power - starts POWER_MAX.  Raising the shield spends SHIELD_PWR_COST;
            the cloak drains CLOAK_DRAIN per second.  No recharge.  Below
            SHIELD_PWR_COST the shield can't go up again, and at 0 the game
            is over (life support needs power).
    Ammo  - starts AMMO_MAX, minus one per torpedo that actually launches.
            At 0, SPACE does nothing.
    Shield - the 0..100 shield level (see the S key).
    Damage - the 0..100 hull-damage metric (see below); its bar fills red.
Fuel / power / ammo floor at 0 and never refill.

Beside those, in the same column: "Lv N" (top line), "Kills NN/MM" (or
"No Aliens" at level 0), then "CLOAKED" while cloaked.

When SHOW_STATUS is 0 (small panels: no status strip) there are NO gauges.
Instead, low-resource warnings appear down the RIGHT edge of the box, over
the starfield: "FUEL"/"PWR"/"AMMO"/"SHLD" turn YELLOW under 20% and RED
under 10% (SHLD only warns while the shield is actually up); "HULL" turns
YELLOW over 70% damage and RED over 85%.

Hull DAMAGE
-----------
A 0..100 metric separate from the shield, starting at 0.  It only rises
while the shield is DOWN: +SHIP_DMG_TORPEDO (10) per alien torpedo through the
hull, +SHIP_DMG_RAM (40) per ram (the alien is wrecked in the crash too).
The crew mends DMG_REPAIR_STEP (1) every DMG_REPAIR_MS (2 s).  At >= DMG_MAX
(100) the rocket is destroyed.  Power reaching 0 also ends the game.

Universe
--------
The whole game lives on one fixed pixel grid, UNIVERSE_SIZE on a side
(10,000 px; int32_t coords - the type could hold ~1e9).  The ship never leaves
the box centre on screen; what moves is its univ_x / univ_y, stepped by
the velocity vector every frame (a float carry keeps the sub-pixel part).
It starts at the exact middle.  The grid wraps.  The live X,Y show near the
bottom-right of the screen.

Enemy aliens
------------
Squares (~ the ship's size) on the universal grid, red normally.  A fresh
one spawns ALIEN_SPAWN_MIN..alien_spawn_max px from the ship on its OWN random
heading and just drifts.  The frame its square first touches the play box ("it
can see us") it stops for SPOT_PAUSE frames, then charges straight at the ship
for good, at its own alien_t.speed (each alien copies the alien_speed default -
the rocket's crawl - at spawn).  A drifter that is never seen and wanders past
ALIEN_CULL_DIST is quietly recycled.  ALIEN_ONSCREEN exist at once; refilled as
they leave until alien_max have been used up.  On screen an alien sits at
box_centre + (its univ pos - the ship's), clipped to the box like everything
else.

alien_max / alien_speed / alien_spawn_max are runtime variables in
space_game.c, meant to be set per chosen difficulty, and so is the bool
alien_ram_and_fire (default false; see the shooter section).  Individual
aliens can also be given a non-default alien_t.speed.

Each alien carries its own 0..100 internal damage.  A player torpedo does
ALIEN_HIT_DMG (60), so it takes TWO hits to reach ALIEN_DMG_MAX (100) and be
destroyed.  Above ALIEN_WOUND_ON (50%) it turns YELLOW ("wounded"); it turns
back RED only once the crew's repair (DMG_REPAIR_STEP every DMG_REPAIR_MS, same
as the hull) brings it below ALIEN_WOUND_OFF (39%) - so at <39% it can safely
eat one more hit (39 + 60 = 99).

Shooters: every ALIEN_SHOOTER_EVERY-th created alien (25%) spawns with
ALIEN_TORPEDOES torpedoes.  While torpedoes > 0 it is FROZEN in place - never
drifts, spots, hunts or wanders (aliens_update() clears `hunting` and bails
before any movement).  All it does is fire, and aliens_fire() only fires while
its square is on the play box with the ship.  (The alien_ram_and_fire flag -
default false, a future high-difficulty knob - removes that freeze, letting a
loaded shooter move / hunt / ram AND keep shooting.)  It can't fire for the first
ALIEN_ARM_MS (2 s) after its square first comes on the box - "loading up" - and
then no more than one shot per re-fire gap: ALIEN_FIRE_MS on level 3, doubled
(2 x, so half the rate) on levels 1-2.
Each shot is a WHITE tracer (ATORPEDO_SPEED, red was too faint on the LCD)
aimed - freshly, every shot - at the ship's universe position at that
instant.  A torpedo's heading is then fixed for its whole life; it never
curves, so nudging the ship forward/back after a shot slips it.

The moment a shooter's clip is EMPTY (torpedoes == 0, shots > 0) it is free to
move: it stops holding position and charges the ship to RAM it - at ANY
difficulty, level 1 included (aliens_update() forces hunting for it and lets it
past the level-1 "never move" gate).  A ram wrecks the alien and, shield down,
adds SHIP_DMG_RAM to the hull.  (Reloading it via the idle nudge puts torpedoes
back > 0, so it freezes and shoots again - only once empty can it charge again.)

A "rookie" shooter is a bad shot that improves: shot 1 aims ALIEN_ROOKIE_MISS
(20) px below the ship, the miss shrinking linearly to 0 by shot
ALIEN_ROOKIE_SHOTS (5) - dead on from then on.  The ramp counts the alien's own
`shots` field, so it is unaffected by clip size or a reload.  Between the
tightening aim, the perfect late shots and the closing ram, a rookie left alone
grinds the hull down toward GAME OVER.

Levels 1-3 seed one rookie on the right box edge at the start
(alien_spawn_rookie()).  Then every IDLE_ROOKIE_MS (30 s) with no key event
run_game() intervenes: if |v| >= FNB_MIN_SPEED it first kicks off a
flip-and-burn (fnb_state = FNB_FLIP) and leaves the timer alone, so the block
re-fires once the ship is stopped; stopped, it either RELOADS the live rookie
(torpedoes += ALIEN_TORPEDOES so it keeps shooting instead of ramming;
next_fire_ms = now; `shots` carries over so the aim ramp does too) or, if none
is alive, calls alien_spawn_rookie().  Only one rookie ever exists:
alien_spawn_rookie() no-ops if a `rookie` alien is alive, and since
aliens_respawn() keeps all ALIEN_ONSCREEN slots full it recycles a slot (a
non-shooter, preferring one that hasn't locked on) rather than needing a free
one.  aliens_update() removes a `rookie` outright (no flash, no score) the
moment its square leaves the box - e.g. the ship drove out from under it.
Contact:
    player torpedo hits an alien       -> +ALIEN_HIT_DMG to its damage; at
                                         >= ALIEN_DMG_MAX it flashes + is
                                         removed, else it is left wounded
    alien hits an alien               -> both flash, then both removed
    alien torpedo hits the ship, shield UP   -> shield -SHIELD_HIT_TORPEDO
    alien torpedo hits the ship, shield DOWN -> hull damage +SHIP_DMG_TORPEDO
    alien rams the ship (alien always dies), shield UP   -> shield -SHIELD_HIT_RAM
    alien rams the ship (alien always dies), shield DOWN -> hull damage +SHIP_DMG_RAM
Hull damage >= DMG_MAX, power at 0, or the countdown clock hitting 00:00
(GAME_LEN_S of play, all levels), ends the game.
The "Kills NN/MM" counter shows removed / alien_max -
every removal counts, however it died.  After the rocket is gone the screen
shows "GAME OVER" + "press any key to restart" (both centred in the current
BOX_W x BOX_H play box); any key (bar ~, which still reboots to BOOTSEL) goes
back to the start screen.

WIN: on any level with aliens (score >= alien_max, i.e. all alien_max killed)
run_game() clears the screen to a big-text "YOU WON IN" + the time TAKEN as
MM:SS (elapsed play time, counted up from 00:00, capped at GAME_LEN_S), calls
play_win_song() (a blocking ~WIN_SONG_MS victory jingle), shows "Press any key
to continue", then returns to splash_screen().

Fresh aliens never spawn
inside the visible screen (ALIEN_SPAWN_MIN clears the box) - the one
exception is a single "here is your target" alien that levels 1-3 seed at
the right edge of the box (ALIEN_INTRO_X) at a random height off centre
(ALIEN_INTRO_YMIN..MAX, either way) so the player sees one from frame one
but cannot just autopilot straight into it.

Player torpedoes travel in box coordinates at TORPEDO_SPEED (deliberately above
SPEED_MAX so the ship can't overtake its own fire).  Alien torpedoes travel on
the universe grid at ATORPEDO_SPEED (slower, and dodgeable): fixed heading,
fixed grid track, so if the ship moves off the spot it was aimed at the shot
sails past.  Neither kind is carried by the starfield scroll.

Model
-----
The ship never leaves the centre of the box; only its heading changes.  It
carries a velocity VECTOR with real inertia and no drag - in vacuum it just
coasts.

UP adds a THRUST_IMPULSE-long vector along the current nose direction to the
velocity (true vector addition): thrust one way, turn, thrust again and the
new course is the sum of the pushes, its length the resulting speed, capped
at SPEED_MAX.

DOWN is the exact mirror: it adds BRAKE_IMPULSE (half a THRUST_IMPULSE)
along the OPPOSITE of the nose.  It is NOT a brake - from a standstill it
drives the ship backwards at half the up-thrust rate.

Nothing is a dedicated "slow down".  Heading (nose) and trajectory (the way
you actually drift - the starfield scrolls the opposite way) are separate.
To bleed off speed you aim a push against your drift: point the nose the way
you are drifting and tap DOWN, OR point it retrograde and tap UP.

Because thrust is not continuous, LEFT / RIGHT turn the hull WITHOUT turning
the existing travel.  The starfield scrolls opposite the velocity vector, so
it keeps drifting - ship pointing wherever it likes - until you slow it.

Autopilot
---------
A toggles it; M also leaves it (back to MANUAL navigation, the default when
autopilot is off - leaving flashes "MANUAL" for MANUAL_TXT_MS).  One "hop"
covers AUTO_DIST px - a play-box - of travel; the universe itself is
continuous, this is just a fixed step size.  In autopilot each ARROW
launches a hop: a strict state march AUTO_TURN -> AUTO_MOVE -> AUTO_BRAKE.
TURN rotates the hull to that screen cardinal (its side attitude jet shows)
and zeroes the velocity; MOVE fires the MAIN engine to AUTO_CRUISE (= two UP
pulses) then coasts; BRAKE fires the FRONT engine only, retro-pulsing to a
dead stop and snapping univ_x/univ_y exactly onto AUTO_DIST.  Once BRAKE
starts the main engine never lights again.  Manual flight keys are
locked while a hop runs (the `manual` gate) - except ENTER, which cancels
the hop and starts a flip-and-burn (the bail-out when the course is headed
into an alien).  J / K hand-rotate the hull between hops (they drive the
same k_left / k_right as a normal LEFT / RIGHT); the banner's second line
reminds you ("press J,K keys to rotate ship") whenever autopilot is on and
idle (it reads "engaged" during a hop, "flip-and-burn" during that).  Runs
out of fuel -> the hop aborts and the ship coasts.

Every star has a depth d in [DEPTH_MIN, 1].  Its scroll step is d * speed,
so near stars (d ~ 1) sweep past fast and far stars (d small) barely move -
that spread is the parallax cue.  Depth also sets brightness (near = white,
far = dim blue-grey).  Once a star's per-frame step is long enough it is
drawn as a streak (a short line back along its travel) instead of a dot,
which is what sells speed at a high cruise.  A star leaving one edge wraps
to the opposite edge with a fresh random position AND a fresh depth, so the
field never visibly repeats.

Rendering
---------
No RAM framebuffer: we draw straight into the ST7789P frame memory with
lcd_solid_rectangle().  Each star stores the exact segment it last drew and
erases that (background colour) before drawing its new dot / streak.  The
ship (tri_fill) and the alien squares (px_rect) are SOLID - drawn and
erased as filled shapes; the flames stay wireframe (tri_outline).  The hull
is COL_SHIP white, COL_SHIP_WOUND yellow once damage > SHIP_WOUND_ON (33) OR
power < SHIP_WOUND_ON percent, or COL_SHIELD cyan while the shield is up (cyan
wins); COL_SHIP_FLASH on the death flash.
px_rect() clips every in-box write to the interior so nothing can touch the
border or the area outside the box.  The status gauges are the exception - they
sit in that outside area on purpose and write there with raw
lcd_solid_rectangle() / lcd_putstr(), repainting only when their value
changes.

For ~FLAME_HOLD_MS after each UP press a big orange "flame" triangle hangs
off the tail, pointing straight back, its length + colour re-flickering
every frame.  For ~BRAKE_HOLD_MS after each DOWN press two small white
flames show off the front sides instead.  While LEFT / RIGHT is turning the
hull a small white attitude jet (rcs_verts) flares off ONE front side - the
side opposite the turn, so its thrust yaws the nose the way you asked.
Order per frame: erase old ship body + old flames/jet -> move/redraw stars
-> draw flames/jet -> draw the alien squares -> draw the ship body on top
(so it covers any star that ran under it, and the flames appear to come out
from under it).

Long Range Sensors: when an alien is off the visible box but its screen X
or Y still falls inside a box edge (i.e. it is straight up / down / left /
right of the box, not diagonally away), a small red triangular blip is
drawn just inside that edge pointing at it.  On-screen aliens and purely
diagonal ones get no blip.

HINTS (DIFFICULTY 0 AND 1 - the two "with hints" levels): grey (COL_HINT)
big-text captions along the bottom of the box, each armed for HINT_MS (2 s).
The hint block runs for level <= 1; the alien survey and the level-1 hints are
gated inside it.  Only one caption uses the row at a time; priority is
move > next-alien > shields > fire > quit.
  - "Arrow Keys to Move": levels 0 AND 1, shown once as the game starts.
  - "Press Q to Quit": level 0 ONLY, shown at the 1-minute and 2-minute marks
    (HINT_Q_AT_1_MS / HINT_Q_AT_2_MS).
  - "Next Alien This Way" (level 1): the play box holds NO alien but some are
    still alive.  Also a grey isosceles triangle centred on the box - apex
    HINT_ARROW_TIP px toward the nearest off-box alien, base HINT_ARROW_BACK the
    other way, 2*HINT_ARROW_HALF wide - redrawn / erased each frame so it tracks
    the ship.  Re-armed whenever the box is cleared again, and whenever a
    flip-and-burn ENDS with the box still empty.
  - "Use S for Shields" (level 1): armed the frame the hull first becomes
    "wounded" (damage > SHIP_WOUND_ON, or power < that percent) with no shield
    up; re-armed once that clears.
  - "SPACE key to fire" (level 1): shown ONCE, HINT_FIRE_AFTER_MS (30 s) into
    the game, if no torpedo has been fired yet (ammo still == AMMO_MAX).  Does
    not re-arm.

Audio
-----
The starter audio driver is a single square-wave voice per channel with a
100..2115 Hz range and no volume control.  Four short sounds, in priority
order (highest wins the voice):
  - "alien destroyed" chime: a rising 3-note major arpeggio
    (CHIME_N1 -> N2 -> N3, split evenly over CHIME_MS ~220 ms), armed the frame
    a player torpedo takes an alien's damage to ALIEN_DMG_MAX.  Beats all;
  - player "pew": a fast pitch DROP PEW_HI -> PEW_LO over PEW_MS (~110 ms);
  - alien "pew": the same chirp REVERSED, PEW_LO -> PEW_HI;
  - thruster "hiss": per-frame random high pitch (HISS_LO..HISS_HI, an
    independent value for L and R) - fake white noise on a square voice -
    while UP / DOWN or a turn is firing a jet and for ~HISS_MS after.
The voice is silent the rest of the time.

play_win_song() is separate: a blocking ~WIN_SONG_MS happy jingle (a static
{Hz, ms} note list; Hz 0 == a rest) walked with sleep_ms().  Played on the
"YOU WON IN" screen before the "press any key" prompt, and once at program
startup on the first splash_screen() (guarded by a static flag).  It drives the
PIO with raw audio_play_sound() rather than the sound_set() latch, so it must
end by calling audio_stop() and zeroing snd_l / snd_r directly - a plain
sound_silence() there no-ops against the stale latch and leaves the last note
ringing until the next sound.

Only lcd, southbridge, font-8x10 and audio from picocalc-text-starter (by
Blair Leduc) are used.
